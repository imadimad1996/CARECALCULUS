# CareCalculus Pricing Model

CareCalculus operates on a freemium model, designed to be accessible to all clinicians worldwide while offering premium workflow features for power users and institutions.

## Free Tier
- **Price:** $0 (Forever Free)
- **Features:** 
  - Full access to all 88+ clinical calculators, formulas, guidelines, and reference tools.
  - Core scoring algorithms and logic.
  - Multilingual support (English, French).

## Pro Export Pass
- **Price:** $1.66 / month (Billed annually at $19.99 / year)
- **Features:**
  - Everything in the Free Tier.
  - Unlimited 1-Click EHR SOAP/SBAR note exports.
  - Copy-paste snippets and advanced clinical export templates.

## Enterprise / Hospitals
- **Price:** Custom pricing
- **Features:**
  - Enterprise integration for health systems.
  - Custom EHR/SMART-on-FHIR workflows.
  - Dedicated support.
